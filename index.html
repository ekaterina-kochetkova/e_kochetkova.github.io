Academic Experience

I am a fourth year CS PhD student at EPFL working with Michael Kapralov. I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science. One direction of my current work is applying spectral graph theory to sublinear algorithm design. Another branch is optimization the memory/runtime of LLM inference, emphasizing algorithms with strong theoretical guarantees.
